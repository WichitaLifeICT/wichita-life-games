Reader submissions

Approved words are accepted as guesses on the site right away. To put them into the daily answer rotation, paste this over the WHEATLE_WORDS array in index.html and re-upload. It's a manual step on purpose — see the note below.

Why this isn't automatic: the day's answer is picked by counting into the word list, so quietly growing the list would change today's word for anyone mid-game. Promoting words on your own schedule keeps a day's puzzle the same for everybody who plays it.
